Risks & Privacy

Smart Technology Requires Trust.

Smart Stride creates value through AI and data, but it also needs clear privacy protection, responsible advice, and reliable technology.

Data Privacy

Running data, location, and performance information must be protected.

AI Accuracy

AI analysis may not always be correct.

Injury Liability

Wrong advice could increase injury risk.

Connectivity Issues

Bluetooth, sensors, or app syncing may fail.

High Price

Smart technology may make the product less affordable.

Race Fairness

The product may be considered unfair in competitive races.

How We Reduce These Risks

  • Use data encryption and clear privacy policies.
  • Explain that AI advice is guidance, not medical diagnosis.
  • Provide regular software updates.
  • Offer customer support for technical problems.
