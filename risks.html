Risks & Privacy

Risks and Privacy

Smart technology brings convenience, but it also creates privacy, health, and fairness concerns.

Main Concern

Data Privacy

Smart Stride collects running data, performance data, and possibly location data. This information must be protected to maintain customer trust.

AI Accuracy

The AI analysis may not always be accurate.

Wrong Advice

If the product gives wrong advice, users may change their running style in a harmful way.

Health and Injury Risk

Incorrect advice could increase injury risk.

IoT and Connection Problems

Bluetooth, sensors, app syncing, or internet connection may fail.

Race Fairness

Smart Stride could be considered unfair or cheating in some competitive races.

High Price

The smart technology may make the shoes less affordable for some customers.

How Smart Stride Reduces Risks

  • Clear privacy policy
  • Data encryption
  • AI advice disclaimer
  • Regular software updates
  • Customer support
  • Users should not treat AI advice as medical advice
