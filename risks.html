Transparency

Risks and Privacy

Smart technology brings benefits, but it also creates important responsibilities. We believe in being transparent about both.

🔒

Data Privacy

Running data, location, and performance information must be protected from unauthorised access and misuse.

🤖

AI Accuracy

AI analysis may not always be correct. Recommendations should be used as guidance, not medical advice.

⚕️

Injury Liability

Wrong advice could increase injury risk. Users should consult professionals for health concerns.

📶

Connectivity Issues

Bluetooth, sensors, or app syncing may occasionally fail or produce incomplete data.

💰

High Price

Smart technology may make the product less affordable compared to traditional running shoes.

🏁

Race Fairness

The product may be considered unfair in competitive races and could face regulatory restrictions.

Our Commitment

How We Address These Risks

✓
Clear privacy policy explaining exactly what data we collect and how it is used.
✓
End-to-end data encryption for all personal and running data.
✓
AI advice disclaimer clearly stating recommendations are not medical advice.
✓
Regular software updates to improve AI accuracy and fix known issues.
✓
Dedicated customer support team available for product and safety questions.
