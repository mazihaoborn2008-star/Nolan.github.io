Behavioral

Targeting

Concentrated Niche Marketing

Smart Stride focuses on runners and fitness users who want smart, high-quality running shoes. Instead of targeting everyone, the brand focuses on a specific niche: people who want comfort, performance tracking, and injury prevention in one product.

Positioning

Shoe and Tracker in One

  • No need to wear a sports watch or extra tracker
  • Combines comfort with smart running analysis
  • Identifies poor running style
  • Helps prevent injuries
  • Makes running simpler and more convenient
  • Cheaper than buying running shoes and a sports watch separately
